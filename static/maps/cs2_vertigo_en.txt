Vertigo is a unique defusal map set on the top floors of a skyscraper under construction. The map is defined by its strong verticality and is divided into two distinct levels. Gameplay is characterized by very fast-paced engagements, an abundance of scaffolding, and the constant risk of falling off the edge of the building. Due to the proximity of the bomb sites to the defensive positions, sound cues and aggressive utility usage are critical for holding off rushes on Vertigo.
